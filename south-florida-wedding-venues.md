# South Florida Wedding Venues — Full Buyout & BYO Catering

## What We're Looking For (Baseline: Coral Gables Country Club)
- **Full day buyout** — exclusive use of the venue for the entire day
- **BYO catering** — bring your own caterer, not locked into in-house or a short preferred list
- **Full customization** — make it your own event (decor, vendors, timeline, etc.)
- **Location:** Palm Beach County south through Miami-Dade
