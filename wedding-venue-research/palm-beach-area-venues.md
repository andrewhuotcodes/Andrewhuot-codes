## PART 1: Flagler Museum - Detailed Analysis

### Overview

The Henry Morrison Flagler Museum (Whitehall) is a National Historic Landmark in Palm Beach, built in 1902 as Henry Flagler's Gilded Age mansion. The museum offers a fairytale-like setting combining historic grandeur with modern event spaces.

**Location:** 1 Whitehall Way, Palm Beach, FL 33480

### Capacity & Guest Count

- **Overall Capacity:** Up to 250 guests for wedding ceremonies and receptions (Note: Some sources indicate 160 guests for combined ceremony and reception in certain spaces)
- **Coconut Palm Tree Walk (Outdoor Ceremony):** Up to 300 guests
- **West Room Terrace (Outdoor):** Up to 100 guests
- **Flagler Kenan Pavilion (Reception):** Primary space for seated dinners and dancing, accommodates larger guest counts comfortably

The museum features 3 refined grand halls and 2 massive courtyards to support up to 250 guests across all available spaces.

### Ceremony Locations

**1. The Courtyard**
- Most popular and picturesque spot for outdoor ceremonies
- Features a stunning fountain centerpiece
- Surrounded by classical architecture
- Romantic, classical ambiance

**2. Grand Hall (Indoor)**
- Soaring ceilings and marble columns
- Incredibly dramatic and opulent backdrop
- Ideal for a grander, indoor ceremony

**3. Coconut Palm Tree Walk**
- Outdoor ceremony space
- Capacity: Up to 300 guests
- Scenic palm-lined walkway

**4. West Room Terrace**
- Outdoor option
- Capacity: Up to 100 guests

### Reception Spaces

**Flagler Kenan Pavilion** (Primary Reception Space)
- Modern addition constructed in 2005
- Climate-controlled, flexible space
- High ceilings and large windows
- Overlooks the Intracoastal Waterway
- Houses Flagler's private railcar, "Railcar No. 91"
- Designed to complement the historic building
- Best suited for seated dinners and dancing

**Grand Hall** (Cocktail Hour)
- Frequently used for cocktail receptions
- Allows guests to mingle amidst historic splendor

**Lake Room** (Cocktail Hour Alternative)
- Beautiful views of the Intracoastal Waterway
- More intimate and serene setting
- Alternative cocktail hour space
