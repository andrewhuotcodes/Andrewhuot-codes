This comprehensive guide covers 15 luxury wedding venues across West Coast Florida suitable for 250 guests in April 2027. April is an ideal time for a Florida Gulf Coast wedding—Easter (March 28, 2027) and most spring breaks will have concluded, providing excellent weather (70-88°F) with reduced tourist crowds compared to peak winter season.

## APRIL 2027 WEDDING PLANNING CONSIDERATIONS

### Weather
- **Temperature:** 70-88°F (daytime), high 60s to mid-70s (evening)
- **Conditions:** Mostly sunny, very little rain
- **Best time:** April is one of the best months for Florida Gulf Coast weddings
- Perfect for beach ceremonies and outdoor receptions

### Tourist Season Timing
- **Easter 2027:** Sunday, March 28
- **Spring Break:** Most colleges March 21-28, some through early April
- **April Advantage:** Post-Easter and post-spring break timing means:
  - Reduced tourist crowds compared to February-March peak
  - Better availability
  - Potentially better pricing than winter peak season
  - Still excellent weather

### Peak Season Notes
- February-May is prime Florida wedding season
- April is shoulder season (spring) with thinning winter crowds
- Book early (12+ months in advance)
- Hotels and resorts have premium rates, but lower than Easter weekend
