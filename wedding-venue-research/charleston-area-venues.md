## April Wedding Considerations in Charleston

**Weather:** April in Charleston features comfortable temperatures ranging from mid-60s to high 70s (58°F-72°F), ideal for outdoor ceremonies and receptions. Spring offers gorgeous natural light with longer days, low humidity, and crisp, clear air perfect for photography.

**Azalea Season:** Charleston's famous azaleas, magnolias, and wisteria are in full bloom during April, creating breathtaking photo opportunities with walls of pink azaleas. Peak bloom typically occurs in March and April, though exact timing can vary.

**Planning Considerations:**
- Spring (March-May) is peak wedding season in Charleston - book early
- Pollen can be intense in late March and April - have tissues and allergy medication available
- Have backup plans for outdoor ceremonies in case of rain
- Garden venues like Middleton Place and Magnolia Plantation are especially stunning during azalea season
