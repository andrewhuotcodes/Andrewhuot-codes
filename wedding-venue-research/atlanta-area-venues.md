# Atlanta Area Wedding Venues - April 2027
## Research for 250-Guest Wedding

---

## Executive Summary

This comprehensive guide covers 15 premier wedding venues in the Atlanta metropolitan area suitable for hosting 250 guests in April 2027. Atlanta's spring season offers ideal weather (average temperatures between 51°F and 71°F) with stunning blooms including azaleas, dogwoods, tulips, and peonies. April is a peak wedding month, so booking well in advance is essential.

**Important April Considerations:**
- Beautiful spring blooms at peak season
- Comfortable temperatures (51°F-71°F)
- Higher possibility of rain - backup indoor spaces are essential
- Peak pricing applies for April weddings at most venues
- Pollen levels can be high during Atlanta spring season

## April Weather Planning Guide for Atlanta

### Temperature & Comfort
- **Average April Temperatures:** 51°F - 71°F
- **Daytime:** Comfortable 65°F - 75°F range
- **Evening:** Can drop to 50°F - plan for guest comfort
- **Recommendations:**
  - Light, airy fabrics (chiffon, satin, crepe) for bridesmaids
  - Consider pashminas or blankets for evening outdoor events
  - Heaters for outdoor reception areas if needed

### Rain Considerations
- April is known for rain showers in Atlanta
- **Essential:** Indoor backup plan for any outdoor ceremony
- Choose venues with versatile indoor/outdoor options
- Discuss rain plans with venue coordinator early
- Consider clear-top tents for outdoor receptions
- Have umbrellas available for guests

### Seasonal Beauty
- **Peak Blooms:**
  - Azaleas (early April)
  - Dogwoods (throughout April)
  - Tulips
  - Peonies
  - Gardenias
  - Fresh spring greenery
- Beautiful natural décor built into spring venues
- Fresh, romantic spring aesthetic
- Photography opportunities with blooming gardens

### Pollen Considerations
- Spring pollen can be high in Atlanta
- Consider:
  - Having allergy medication available
  - Indoor cocktail hour if pollen is severe
  - Timing outdoor events for late afternoon when pollen decreases
  - Informing guests with known allergies

### Venue Selection Tips for April
- **Must-Have:** Backup indoor space for ceremonies
- **Ideal:** Venues with both indoor and outdoor options
- **Consider:** Garden venues at peak bloom beauty
- **Book Early:** April is peak wedding season - reserve 12-18 months ahead

1. **April Specific:**
