# NYC Area Wedding Venues - April 2027 Wedding (250 Guests)

## Executive Summary

This comprehensive guide presents 15 premier wedding venues in the NYC area capable of accommodating 250 guests for an April 2027 wedding. April is considered a transitional month in NYC's wedding calendar - some venues classify it as peak season while others consider it late off-season, potentially offering cost advantages compared to summer/fall. Weather is generally mild (highs around 64°F) with the added bonus of cherry blossom season.

**Price Range Overview**: $180 - $550 per person
**Total Budget Range**: $45,000 - $250,000+

## April 2027 Wedding Considerations

### Weather
- **Average Temperature**: High 64°F, Low 45°F
- **Precipitation**: Approximately 8 rainy days per month (similar to other months)
- **Key Advantage**: Cherry blossom season (late March - early April) at venues like Brooklyn Botanic Garden and Central Park
- **Recommendation**: Have indoor backup plans for outdoor ceremonies, but weather is generally cooperative

### Pricing & Peak Season Status
April occupies a unique position in NYC's wedding calendar:

- **Some venues classify April as PEAK SEASON** (April-December), resulting in higher pricing
  - Examples: Liberty House, Hudson House (standard season)

- **Other venues classify April as OFF-PEAK or SHOULDER SEASON** (November-April), offering savings
  - Examples: Crescent Beach Club, potentially some Long Island venues
